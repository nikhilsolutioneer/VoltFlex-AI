**EKZ Flexibility: Team Discussion Draft**

**Goal**
Customer savings first; explain wider grid and climate impacts separately. Cheaper does not always benefit either.

**Household Model**
- Infer from 15-minute usage; customers confirm/correct or say "not sure". No archetype selection.
- Single-family and multi-family homes have different consumption profiles, metering and control rights.
- Both can combine PV, heat pumps, electric water heaters, EVs, lighting, appliances and batteries.
- Flexibility varies by direction, power, energy, timing and constraints. PV can be curtailed, not raised above available solar output; loads can shift within limits; batteries charge/discharge.

**Inputs & Platform**
Import readings, tariff rates/eligibility and EKZ signals: available, not yet reviewed. Rayfin on Microsoft Fabric.

**Proposed Journey**
1. **Understand:** recognise patterns and impact; refine equipment, routines and constraints.
2. **Explore:** compare behaviour, eligible tariffs and investments against current and future usage.
3. **Act:** choose a trial, tariff decision, investment assessment or flexibility-programme next step.

Act is the shared ending. Results review is a later return visit, outside the first examples.

**First UI Examples**
- **My Electricity + My Impact:** chart, assumptions, grid effects, estimated emissions and collective-action scenarios.
- **My Opportunities:** savings, effort, grid effects.
- **Compare an Option:** baseline vs scenario, costs, uncertainty.
- **My Next Step:** action and reason.

Optional chat + Energy Momentum story beside the analysis.

**Boundaries**
Label demo data, inferences and impact estimates. No live switching, enrollment or device control. Include limited flexibility and investment budgets; state assumptions and avoid double counting.

**Team Feedback**
- What need or constraint is missing?
- Which household and equipment combination should we prototype?
- Is the next step credible and useful?
